The Musk Noise Report

There is always noise around Elon Musk. This sorts one week of it into threads, and separates things that happened from things he posted.

Show:

Happened

A Musk-backed PAC texted Colorado voters about ballots they didn't need to request

Election officials say America PAC, Musk's super PAC, texted voters in Weld and Adams counties telling them their ballot request was in and asking them to confirm where and when they would return it. Every active registered voter in Colorado already gets a mail ballot automatically, so the texts set off calls from confused residents.

Colorado's Secretary of State called the messaging "misleading." A PAC spokesman did not respond to questions about the Colorado texts; one Republican strategist called the activity standard get-out-the-vote tactics.

Why it matters Wrong instructions about how to vote are the one kind of campaign noise that can cost someone their vote.

As reported by CNN, Oct. 10, 2026.

HappenedPosted

A documentary called Musk goes nationwide, and its subject is not a fan

Alex Gibney's documentary Musk opens nationwide this week. Over the past few weeks Musk has attacked Gibney on X, calling him an "idiot" and a "horrible human."

The film's distributor says it has not been able to run paid advertisements on several major social media platforms.

Why it matters The posts are noise; whether a film about a platform owner can buy ads on platforms is the part worth watching.

As reported by the Associated Press, Oct. 12, 2026.

Posted

He told three Canadian provinces to leave Canada

Musk posted that Quebec, Alberta, and Saskatchewan should separate from Canada. Alberta Premier Danielle Smith, Saskatchewan Premier Scott Moe, and Prime Minister Mark Carney quickly rejected the remarks.

Why it matters Mostly noise: a post, swiftly rebuffed. The open question, which the coverage asks too, is whether it was trolling or part of something larger.

As reported by CBC News.

Starlink and Tesla stall in India

Plans for Starlink and Tesla in India have faltered amid persistent regulatory hurdles. This week Musk posted that powerful corporate interests in India were blocking Starlink, calling it "a crime against the people of India."

Why it matters The business story is the stall; the post is the reaction to it.

As reported by Bloomberg, Oct. 9, 2026.

How this was made

Happened means a news outlet reported an event involving other people or institutions. Posted means the story is, at least in part, something Musk wrote online. Many stories are both: the post is the reaction, the event is the news.

Every claim here is attributed to the outlet that reported it and links to that report. Nothing was added beyond what those reports say. This is a snapshot from one week; by the time you read it, the noise has moved on.
